Privacy policy

Last updated September 28, 2026

Mealwright is an iPhone app that plans a household's dinners. This policy says what the app keeps, why, where, for how long, and how to have it removed. The short version: we keep only what's needed to plan your meals and share it with your household, we never sell it or use it for advertising, and you can ask us to delete it at any time. Allergies, intolerances, and diets are health information, and our consumer health data policy covers them in more detail.

What we collect

  • Your sign-in. Adults sign in with Apple. We receive an account identifier. Apple may offer your name the first time you sign in; Mealwright doesn't keep it. We don't ask Apple for your email address, and we never see your Apple password.
  • Your household. The first names or nicknames you give the people in your home, children included, and each person's food preferences, allergies, intolerances, and diets.
  • What you plan and cook. Your menu, recipes (including ones you import from a link, text, or photo), recipe versions, ratings, cooking times, who's cooking which night, your kitchen inventory, your grocery list, and a record of grocery orders you mark as placed.
  • What you type, say, or photograph in the app. Requests like “swap Friday for tacos”, spoken lists (turned into text on your phone), and photos of a shelf or recipe, which are used to answer or carry out the request.
  • Products you pick at a store. When you match a list item to a product at a retailer, we remember that product link so it's suggested next time.

We don't collect your location or contacts, and the app has no advertising or third-party analytics.

How we use it

  • To plan meals that are safe and suitable for everyone in your household, and to build your recipes and grocery list.
  • To keep everyone in your household in sync. Adults who join your household see and edit the same plan, kitchen, and list.
  • To keep track of how much AI processing each household uses, so the service stays affordable.

We don't sell your information, share it with advertisers, or use it to build profiles for anyone else. We don't use it to train AI models, and our AI provider's terms don't allow it to either.

Where it's kept, and who processes it

  • Supabase hosts our database and sign-in service, in the United States. Your household's data is readable only by members of your household. The database enforces this, not just the app. Data travels between your phone and our servers encrypted.
  • Anthropic provides the AI model that plans meals, writes and changes recipes, answers questions, and reads photos and lists. The parts of your household's information a request needs, such as who eats what and who is allergic to what, are sent to Anthropic's API through our own server. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, API inputs aren't used to train its models, and Anthropic deletes them within 30 days (longer only if they are flagged for a safety review). Photos are processed to answer the request and aren't stored by Mealwright.
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple, notifications, and app distribution.
  • Your phone keeps a copy of your household's data so the app works quickly and offline.

Shopping and retailers

When you shop, Mealwright opens the retailer's own website (for example Walmart, or Publix through Instacart) inside the app, or hands your list to the retailer as a cart link. You sign in to the retailer and check out on their site, under their privacy policy. Mealwright doesn't see or store your retailer password or payment details. Some cart links are affiliate links, which tell the retailer that Mealwright referred the purchase and may earn Mealwright a commission, at no cost to you.

How long we keep it

  • Your household's data stays for as long as you use Mealwright.
  • What you ask the AI isn't kept by Mealwright, except where it becomes part of your household's own record, such as a recipe's change history. Anthropic deletes it within 30 days, as above.
  • Records of AI use (which household and person, which task, and how much processing) are kept while your household exists. They hold no content.
  • When you ask us to delete your account, or your whole household and everything in it, we delete it from our database within 30 days and confirm when it's done. Deleting the app from your phone removes the phone's copy.

Children

Mealwright is a family app. An adult sets up each person in the household, children included, with a first name or nickname and what they eat, so dinners suit everyone. That information comes from the adult, not the child.

An adult can also invite a child in the household to use Mealwright with their own sign-in. A child who signs in can see the household's menu and recipes, suggest dinners (an adult decides whether to add them), rate dinners they ate, keep their likes and dislikes, mark a dinner cooked, and use the kitchen and grocery list. What a child adds is seen only by their household and is never public. A child can't shop, change settings, or change what the adults planned.

From a child who signs in, we collect an account identifier, what they add in the app, and what they type in the message box. Our AI provider, Anthropic, reads what they type in order to answer them, as it does for adults, and doesn't use it to train its models. We don't ask children for their email address, birthdate, location, or photos of themselves, and no advertising or third-party analytics run in the app.

Children under 13 may have their own sign-in only with a parent's verifiable consent. Sign in with Apple and Apple's TestFlight aren't available to children under 13, and we don't knowingly let a child under 13 sign in without a parent's consent. If we learn that one has, we delete what they added. We're adding a way for a parent to give consent and a sign-in for kids that needs no Apple account or email; this policy will describe both before they're used.

Teenagers use Mealwright within their household in the same way, with the same protections: no advertising, no sale of their data, and nothing designed to keep them in the app.

A parent can see what their child has added in the app, and can ask us at any time to stop their child's sign-in or to delete their child's information, by contacting us (below).

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how your information is used, we'll say so in the app first, and if it affects how children's information is used, we'll ask their parents first.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub. Please don't put personal details in an issue, since issues are public; say what you need and we'll arrange the rest with you privately.
