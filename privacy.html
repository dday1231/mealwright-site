Privacy policy

Last updated September 17, 2026

Mealwright is an iPhone app that plans a household's dinners. This policy says what the app keeps, why, where, and how to have it removed. The short version: we keep what's needed to plan your meals and share it with your household, we never sell it or use it for advertising, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

What we collect

  • Your sign-in. You sign in with Apple. We receive an account identifier and, if you choose to share it, an email address (which may be Apple's private relay address). We never see your Apple password.
  • Your household. The names you give the people in your home, and each person's food preferences, allergies, intolerances, and diets. Children appear only as profiles an adult creates. They have no accounts of their own.
  • What you plan and cook. Your menu, recipes (including ones you import from a link, text, or photo), recipe versions, ratings, cooking times, who's cooking which night, your kitchen inventory, your grocery list, and a record of grocery orders you mark as placed.
  • What you type, say, or photograph in the app. Requests like “swap Friday for tacos”, spoken lists (turned into text on your phone), and photos of a shelf or recipe, which are used to answer or carry out the request.
  • Products you pick at a store. When you match a list item to a product at a retailer, we remember that product link so it's suggested next time.

We don't collect your location or contacts, and the app has no advertising or third-party analytics.

How we use it

  • To plan meals that are safe and suitable for everyone in your household, and to build your recipes and grocery list.
  • To keep everyone in your household in sync. Adults who join your household see and edit the same plan, kitchen, and list.
  • To keep track of how much AI processing each household uses, so the service stays affordable.

We don't sell your information, share it with advertisers, or use it to build profiles for anyone else.

Where it's kept, and who processes it

  • Supabase hosts our database and sign-in service, in the United States. Your household's data is readable only by members of your household. The database enforces this, not just the app.
  • Anthropic provides the AI model that plans meals, writes and changes recipes, answers questions, and reads photos and lists. The parts of your household's information a request needs are sent to Anthropic's API through our own server. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, API inputs aren't used to train its models. Photos are processed to answer the request and aren't stored by Mealwright.
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple, notifications, and app distribution.
  • Your phone keeps a copy of your household's data so the app works quickly and offline.

Shopping and retailers

When you shop, Mealwright opens the retailer's own website (for example Walmart, or Publix through Instacart) inside the app, or hands your list to the retailer as a cart link. You sign in to the retailer and check out on their site, under their privacy policy. Mealwright doesn't see or store your retailer password or payment details. Some cart links are affiliate links, which tell the retailer that Mealwright referred the purchase and may earn Mealwright a commission, at no cost to you.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your household's data for as long as you use Mealwright. To delete your account, or your whole household and everything in it, contact us (below) and we'll delete it from our database within 30 days, and confirm when it's done. Deleting the app from your phone removes the phone's copy.

Children

Mealwright is meant to be used by adults. A child's name and food needs are entered by a parent so that meals suit them. Children don't sign in, and we don't knowingly collect information from children directly.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how your information is used, we'll say so in the app first.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub. Please don't put personal details in an issue, since issues are public; say what you need and we'll arrange the rest with you privately.
