Dinner, planned for the whole family.

Mealwright plans the week's dinners around what everyone in your home eats, walks you through each recipe while you cook, remembers what's in your kitchen, and turns the week into one grocery list.

What it does

A menu that keeps itself ahead

Two weeks of dinners, always planned ahead. Change any night by typing or saying it: “swap Friday for tacos”, “Wednesday is leftovers”.

Safe for everyone at the table

Allergies, intolerances, and diets are set per person and checked on every recipe. When one person can't eat the main dish, they get their own version of it.

Recipes you can cook from

Every dinner comes as step-by-step cards with timers. Ask a question mid-cook, or change the recipe in a sentence. Every version is kept.

A kitchen that keeps count

Tell Mealwright what's in the pantry and fridge, or snap a photo of a shelf. Cooking uses it up, and whatever runs out goes on the list.

One list for the week

Everything the week's recipes need, minus what's already in the house, plus anything anyone adds by name. Milk, bananas, paper towels.

Two cooks, one plan

Both adults see and change the same menu, list, and kitchen, in sync. Every change can be undone.

How it works

  1. Tell it who's eating.Each person's likes, allergies, and diet, and your weekly rhythm, like “Wednesday is leftovers”.
  2. Get a week of dinners.Mealwright suggests meals you'll actually cook, and learns which ones your family loves.
  3. Shop once.The list covers the week. Send it to your grocer and check out there, as you normally would.
  4. Cook with the cards.Step by step, with timers that ring like an alarm, even with the phone muted.

Shopping with your grocer

Mealwright helps you fill your cart at the stores you already use. Today that's Walmart, and Publix through Instacart. Mealwright never checks out for you: you review the cart and place the order with the store, on their site, with your own account.

When Mealwright sends your list to a retailer, it may use that retailer's affiliate links. If you buy through one, Mealwright may earn a small commission, at no extra cost to you. It doesn't change which products are suggested.

Private by design

No ads, no pop-ups, and no selling your data. Your household's information is used to plan your meals and nothing else. Read the privacy policy.
