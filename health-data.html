Consumer health data policy

Last updated September 28, 2026

Some of what you tell Mealwright is health information: the allergies, intolerances, and diets you record for the people in your household. Washington's My Health My Data Act, and similar laws in Nevada and Connecticut, call this “consumer health data.” This page says what we collect, why, who handles it, and what you can do. Our privacy policy covers everything else.

What we collect

  • Allergies and intolerances for each person in your household, such as a peanut allergy or lactose intolerance.
  • Diets, such as vegetarian, gluten-free, halal, or kosher. Some diets can reveal a health condition or a religious belief.
  • What follows from them inside the app, such as a warning that a recipe contains something a person is allergic to.

An adult in the household enters this for each person, or a person enters it for themselves. We don't get it from anywhere else.

Why we collect it

Only to plan meals that are safe for the people who eat them: to leave out foods someone is allergic to, to warn when a recipe or the grocery list contains one, and to cook a version for each diet. It's needed to provide the meal planning you ask Mealwright for, and we use it for nothing else. We don't use it for advertising, we don't sell it, and we don't use it to train AI models.

Who handles it

  • Your household. The adults and children in your household see each other's allergies and diets, so everyone knows what's safe.
  • Supabase stores it for us, in the United States, readable only by your household.
  • PowerSync (Journey Mobile, Inc.) passes it from our database to your household's signed-in phones, on our behalf, and to no one else's.
  • Anthropic, our AI provider, receives the allergies and diets a request needs, so every suggestion accounts for them. Anthropic processes it only for us, doesn't train its models on it, and deletes it within 30 days.

We don't share it with anyone else, and nobody receives it for advertising.

What you can do

  • See it and change it at any time in the app's settings, under People: an adult in the household can change anyone's, and a child sees their own under My food. Or ask us and we'll do it for you.
  • Ask us to confirm what health data we hold about you or a person in your household, for a copy of it along with the list of companies that handle it for us, or to delete it.
  • Withdraw your consent, if you gave it for anything beyond planning your meals.

We answer requests within 45 days and delete within 30 days of a confirmed request; copies in backups are gone within 6 months. If we can't do what you ask, we'll tell you why, and you can appeal by replying to that answer. If we turn down your appeal, you can contact your state's attorney general.

Contact

Requests: open an issue on GitHub and say what you need, without personal details (issues are public); we'll arrange the rest with you privately.
