For Mealime users

Mealime is closing. Keep your favorites.

Mealime shuts down on October 21, 2026, and deletes everyone's data that day, with no way to export it. Here's how to save what you'd miss before then, and where Mealwright fits if you're looking for a new planner.

Before October 21: save what you'd miss

  1. Recipes from Mealime's collection.Open each favorite on mealime.com and save the page: in Safari, tap Share, then Save to Files, or Print, then Save as PDF. Save its link too. Mealime's recipe pages use the standard recipe format that most recipe apps can import from a link (Mealwright can too), but only while the pages stay up, and Mealime hasn't said they will after October 21.
  2. Recipes you added yourself.They aren't on mealime.com, so copy them out now: a screenshot of each, or the text pasted into Notes.
  3. This week's plan and grocery list.Take screenshots, so the week you're in doesn't disappear halfway through.

Mealime's own successor is Meals Hub, in the apps of Albertsons Companies' stores: Safeway, Albertsons, Vons, Jewel-Osco, Acme, Shaw's, and their sister stores. If that's where you shop, it may be all you need. It plans for that chain's stores.

Where Mealwright fits

Mealwright is a weekly dinner planner for families, built by a dad for his own family's dinners. It's in private testing on iPhone now and opens to everyone in January 2027. iPhone only for now; Android comes later.

Planned for everyone at the table

Each person's allergies, intolerances, diets, and dislikes, checked on every recipe. Someone who can't eat the main dish gets their own version of it.

One list, any store

The week's list, minus what's already in your kitchen. One tap fills a Walmart cart; for any other store, the list comes sorted by aisle, to check off as you shop.

Your recipes come with you

Bring favorites in from a link, pasted text, or a photo of the page. And we're building an export of everything, so you're never stuck like this again.

Two cooks, one plan

Both adults see and change the same week, list, and kitchen, in sync. No ads, no pop-ups, and we never sell your data.

Join the waitlist

We'll invite a few Mealime planners into the beta this fall, and email everyone when Mealwright opens in January. That's all we'll send, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

We use your email only for the beta and the launch, and never sell it. The privacy policy says how we keep it.
